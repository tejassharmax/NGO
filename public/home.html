Every child's health record, in one place.

Ayusha Health is a health record system for children's homes. Staff record each child's checkups, prescriptions, blood tests, vaccinations and documents, and every record is kept in sync with the home's own Google Sheets and Google Drive.

One profile per child

A complete, ordered health history for every child in the home's care.

Reports on demand

A monthly checkup register and a date range report, ready for district inspections.

In the home's own Google account

Workbooks and uploaded documents are created in the home's Google Sheets and Drive.

Who it is for

Ayusha Health is used by the staff of registered children's homes (child care institutions) in India to keep the health records they are required to maintain. It is not a public service: only staff whose email address a home has authorised can sign in.

Why the app asks for Google access

  • Sign in with Google: to confirm who the staff member is.
  • Google Sheets: to create and update the home's health record workbooks (master directory, medical records, monthly checkup register, blood test reports).
  • Google Drive: to store documents uploaded in the app, such as medical reports, in a folder the app creates in the home's Drive. The app only accesses files it created.

Data from Google is used only to provide these features. It is never sold or used for advertising. Read the privacy policy for details.
