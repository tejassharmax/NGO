Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Ayusha Health ("the app", available at ayushahealth.in) is a health record system for children's homes. It is provided by Empowered Margins Foundation ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, where it is stored and who can see it.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Each children's home that uses Ayusha Health decides what is recorded about the children in its care and authorises which staff may sign in. The home is responsible for the records it keeps; we operate the app on the home's behalf and process the records only to provide the service to that home.

2. Information we handle

About children, entered by the home's authorised staff:

  • Profile: name, date of birth, gender, blood group, identity number (such as Aadhaar), guardian's name and contact number, height and weight, medical conditions, allergies, current medications and dental remarks.
  • Health records: clinical checkups (date, temperature, blood pressure, weight, pulse, oxygen saturation, complaint, prescription, eye check, clinic and doctor), blood test reports, appointments and vaccinations, prescriptions, and documents the staff upload.

About staff: the name, email address and profile picture of the Google account used to sign in, and a log of actions in the app (for example "checkup added").

Children do not use the app and do not sign in to it.

3. Information we receive from Google

When staff sign in, or when a home's administrator connects its Google account, the app requests only these permissions:

  • openid and email: to identify the staff member or administrator and check that the email address has been authorised by the home.
  • Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets): to create and update the home's health record workbooks in its own Google account, and to read back the roster sheet the app created.
  • Google Drive, files created by the app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): to store uploaded documents in a folder the app creates, and to share the app's own files with the home's authorised staff. The app cannot see any other files in the Drive.

Ayusha Health's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. People at our organisation do not read it, except when the home asks for help with its own data, for security purposes, or where the law requires it.

4. How we use the information

  • To show each child's health history to the home's authorised staff.
  • To produce the registers and reports the home needs, including for government inspections.
  • To keep the home's Google Sheets and Drive up to date with its records.
  • To keep the service secure and working (for example, rejecting sign-ins from unauthorised accounts).

5. Where the information is stored

  • Google Cloud Firestore, in Google's Mumbai region (asia-south1). Each home's records are kept separately from every other home's.
  • The home's own Google account: the spreadsheets and uploaded documents the app creates there belong to the home.

Our application server, hosted by MilesWeb, passes requests between the app and Google's services. It does not store the records.

6. Who can see the information

  • Only staff whose email address the home has authorised. A home can never see another home's records.
  • Google, as the provider of the infrastructure that stores the data, under its own terms.
  • Government authorities, only when the home itself shares its reports with them, or when the law requires us to.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Every request is checked against a Google-signed sign-in token and the home's list of authorised staff. The keys that let the app write to a home's Google account are stored on the server only and are never sent to a browser. Database security rules block any browser from reading data directly.

8. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Records are kept for as long as the home uses Ayusha Health. Authorised staff can edit or delete records in the app at any time. A home can ask us to delete all of its records by writing to the address below; we will do so within 30 days, except where the law requires otherwise. The home can disconnect its Google account in the app's settings, or revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Spreadsheets and documents already created in the home's Google account remain the home's property.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and tell the homes that use the app.
