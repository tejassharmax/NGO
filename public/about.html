About Ayusha Health

Version 1.0

Ayusha Health keeps health records in one place: checkups, prescriptions, blood test reports, vaccinations, appointments and documents. Every record is also kept in sync with the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive, so the records always stay with the people they belong to.

Who it is for

  • Children's homes (child care institutions), whose staff keep the health records they are required to maintain for every child in their care.
  • Families, who keep each family member's medical reports and checkups together in one private space.

Ayusha Health is not a public service. Only people whose email address has been authorised can sign in, and every home or family sees only its own records.

What it does

One profile per person

A complete, ordered health history: checkups, vitals, growth, blood tests and prescriptions.

Google Sheets, kept up to date

A master directory, medical records and blood test workbooks are created and updated in the owner's Google account.

Documents in Google Drive

Uploaded reports are saved in one folder per person in the owner's Google Drive.

Appointments and reports

Appointments with Google Calendar, a monthly checkup register and reports ready for inspections.

Privacy and security

Sign-in uses Google, and every request is checked against the list of authorised people. Records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in India and in the owner's own Google account; they are never sold or used for advertising. Read the privacy policy and terms of service for details.
