# Ask Ayusha (js/assistant.js, css/ask.css)

Scope: the Ask Ayusha workspace only. Mode: Operate. Audience: care-home staff and admins; first shown in demos to the client. Job: tick sources, ask a question about the space's records, read an answer that can be checked. Constraints: answers render as before (text, tables, record links); sandbox only; nothing else in the app changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ask Ayusha is the home's measuring wall. Every question is numbered on a ruler and every answer hangs from its mark. It refuses the centred-greeting chatbot with bubbles and suggestion chips.

OWN-WORLD: a leaf-green rail, an off-white wall tinted green, a yellow ruler with graphite ticks running the wall's full height, graphite pencil marks, coral for the live or latest mark. Manrope for prose; Barlow Condensed for numerals, measures and labels. Yellow tick boxes and a yellow focus ring.

STORY: staff tick sources, ask in plain words, see the question numbered on the ruler, watch the measuring head slide while the sources are read, then read an answer they can check. Growth answers plot everyone on one scale.
